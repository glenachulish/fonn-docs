# Fonn — Support

Fonn is a tune library for players of traditional music: the notation, the
sound of it, the recordings and links that go with a tune, and a way of putting
tunes together into sets — on your iPhone, your iPad and your Mac, and off
the internet.

## Getting help

Email **88_intake.troupes@icloud.com**. Say which device you are on and what
you were doing, and include a screenshot if there is anything to see.

## Common questions

**Where are my tunes kept?**
On your device, in the app's own storage — and, if you switch iCloud on, in your
own iCloud account so your devices share one library. There is no Fonn account
and no Fonn server, and nobody but you can see any of it. See the
[privacy policy](privacy.md).

**How do I get tunes in?**
The **+** button. It takes one thing you choose — a file, a folder, pasted
notation, a photograph of printed music — and works out what it is. You are
shown what it proposes to add before anything is written, and you can change
any row or leave it out.

You can also search thesession.org from inside the app and take the setting you
want.

**Nothing is written to my library without asking?**
No. Every import goes through a review screen first. A tune whose name matches
one you already have is offered as *another setting of it* rather than a
duplicate — which is usually what it is, in this music.

**How do I get the same library on my phone, my iPad and my Mac?**
Switch on iCloud in Settings, on each device, signed in to the same Apple
Account. Your library then keeps itself in step: add a tune on the phone and it
appears on the Mac, rate one on the iPad and the rating is there on both.

It syncs through your own iCloud account, not through any server of mine — see
the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

If you would rather it did not, turn the switch off and that device keeps its
own library to itself.

**Can I still move a library about by hand?**
Yes. Export a `.ceol.json` from Settings and open it on the other device. Tunes,
sets, collections, version groups and set membership all travel. It is also
worth doing occasionally as your own backup, independent of iCloud.

**How do I change the key?**
Open a tune and use the ♭/♯ buttons above the stave. That changes what you see
and hear. If you want to keep it, save it as a new version — the transposed
notes are written out properly, so it reads correctly on its own rather than
only while a display setting happens to be applied.

**A note is wrong in a tune I imported.**
Open it and use *Edit Notes*. Click a note and move it up or down the stave.
When you save you can either correct the tune in place, or keep both as
versions. If the tune is in any sets, the dialog tells you how many now play
the edit.

**How do I build a set?**
On the Mac there is a circle-of-fifths builder: pick a key, pick a tune, and
the keys that follow well from it light up. It plays the set straight through
as you build it, so you can hear the join. You can also switch the circle off
and just add tunes in the order you play them.

**Can I slow a tune down to learn it?**
Yes — the speed control under the music, and you can loop a range of bars to
work on the hard part.

**Can I record myself, or tune my instrument?**
Both. Recording attaches to the tune; the tuner listens and shows you the
pitch. The first time, iOS will ask for the microphone.

**A YouTube link says the video cannot be played.**
Some videos are set by whoever uploaded them to refuse being played inside
other apps. Nothing can be done about that from this end — the card offers to
open it in YouTube instead.

**The app opened with nothing in it.**
That is expected on a new install: Fonn ships empty rather than with somebody
else's tune book in it. Import a file, or search thesession.org, and you are
away.

## What Fonn does not do

No accounts, no subscription, no advertising, no analytics. It works in flight
mode, which is the point — the places people play are often the places with no
signal.
